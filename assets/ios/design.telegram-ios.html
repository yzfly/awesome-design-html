iOS Surface Anatomy

Three surfaces. One unmistakable blue.

Telegram on iOS is white-canvas, glass-blurred, and unhurried. Every screen earns its place through the same rules — generous spacing, 13px bubbles, and a single accent that does all the heavy lifting.

Chats List

Pinned chats float above. Filter pills run across the top — All Chats · Personal · Channels · Bots. Unread counts come in blue #229ED9; muted chats fall back to gray.

Chat Thread

Incoming bubbles are white with a 1px hairline. Outgoing bubbles sit on #E1F0FA — soft sky blue, never saturated. Tail-corner radius drops from 13px to 4px.

Channel

Channel posts get full-width cards on a faint gray canvas. Reactions chip-row docks below. View counts ride on tabular figures — every post is read by hundreds of thousands.

Animated Stickers

Vector emotion at 60fps.

Telegram pioneered TGS — vector animated stickers compressed under 30KB. Premium adds a sister format with fuller motion, particle effects, and synced sound. The sticker drawer is a first-class surface, never a footnote.

Stickers obey their own radius: 18px on cells, no padding on the bubble — they break the bubble grammar on purpose.

cell: 80×80 radius: 18px format: .tgs maxSize: 30KB

Quote Reply

A 2px bar carries the whole context.

Reply to any message and Telegram inlines a quote — sender name in their personal accent, preview text below, then your reply. The vertical bar borrows the sender's color, so threads stay legible without expanding the bubble.

Quote bars use 2px width, 2px radius, sit 6px from the left edge of the bubble padding.

bar: 2px radius: 13px inset: 6px 8px
Telegram Premium

A gold gradient that earns its keep.

Premium is the one place Telegram allows itself a non-blue accent. A linear gold #F6BE3B → #E89A1A appears on the subscription badge, the star icon next to premium usernames, and on the upsell sheet — and nowhere else.

The gold is paired with the same radius vocabulary as the rest of the app, so it reads as a feature flag, not a brand reset.

Design System Reference

The whole vocabulary, in one place.
