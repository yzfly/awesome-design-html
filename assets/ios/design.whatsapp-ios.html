iOS Surface Anatomy

Three surfaces. Two greens. One blue tick.

Every WhatsApp screen earns its position on an iPhone through the same rules — paper-white chat canvas, conversation-tinted bubbles, action green for new things, and a deep teal header that grounds the room.

Chats list

Vertical list with 44pt avatars, name + preview + timestamp. Unread count in #25D366 pill aligned right. Mute icon replaces the badge when notifications are off. Pinned chats sort to top.

Chat thread

Deep teal header. Wallpaper-tinted canvas #ECE5DD. Incoming white bubble, outgoing #DCF8C6 green-tint. Date separators as floating pills. E2E lock notice as a yellow pill on entry.

Status / Updates

Story ring style — green ring on unviewed, gray on viewed. "My status" with + plus chip in #25D366 circle. Below: recent (chronological) and viewed sections, separated by a quiet gray header bar.

End-to-end encryption

A yellow pill, every time you open the room.

Every new chat opens with an encryption notice in a soft warning-yellow pill — a tap reveals the security code modal. The pill never appears mid-conversation; it lives at the top, anchoring the thread to a guarantee.

The lock glyph carries through to the chat info screen, where verifying via QR code is one tap away.

Voice messages

A waveform that listens with you.

Voice messages render as a real waveform — sampled from the actual audio, not a placeholder. The bar darkens as playback advances. Speed-up (1x → 1.5x → 2x) replaces the play icon during playback.

Transcription (iOS 17+) appears below the waveform in a quoted style, available even before the recipient hears it.

bars: real PCM color: #075E54 played alpha: 1.0 unplayed alpha: 0.35
Read receipts

Single → double → blue. A status ladder that doesn't lie.

One gray check = sent to server. Two gray checks = delivered to recipient's device. Two BLUE checks = read. The ladder is rigorously enforced — there is no half-state, no animation between steps, no ambiguity.

Recipients can disable read receipts globally, which makes their last check stay gray. Groups disable read receipts when ≥ 32 members.

Design System Reference

Two greens, one blue, paper-white canvas.

Every token WhatsApp's iOS app reaches for — surface ladder, type ramp, radius scale, components. The bubbles are the lowest-radius non-pill element in the entire app — the corner says "this is the seam where two people meet."

Radius

The chat bubble lives at 7.5px — sharp enough to feel like ink on paper, soft enough to feel friendly. Avatars are perfect circles. The mic FAB is the only "extruded" element with elevated shadow.

0pt

56pt

≤ 2s
