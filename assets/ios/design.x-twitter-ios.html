iOS Surface Anatomy

Three surfaces. One pure black canvas.

X on iOS is the most restrained app on your phone. Black canvas, white ink, hairline borders. Color appears only where it changes state — and it's always the same legacy Twitter blue.

Timeline

Tweet rows separated only by 1px #2F3336 hairlines. Avatar 40pt left-rail. Action row at the bottom — comment / repost / heart / view count / share / bookmark — laid out for thumb economics.

Profile

Banner image 90pt tall. Avatar 80pt drops 32pt into the body. Follow button white pill, top-right. Tab strip Posts · Replies · Highlights · Media · Likes.

Compose

Full-screen sheet. White Post pill stays disabled until you type. 280-char ring fills in legacy Twitter blue, then turns yellow at 260, red over 280.

Bookmarks

The save gesture that's faster than a screenshot.

Tap the bookmark icon and the row becomes a save sheet — Add to Bookmarks, Add to Folder, Share External. The action committed counts up at the bottom of every tweet as a "view count" of bookmarks.

Bookmark icon goes solid blue when saved. Long-press on tweet reveals the same sheet plus translate, copy link, and Grok this post.

Repost vs Quote

Two amplifications, one button.

Tap the repost icon and a bottom sheet asks: pure repost, or quote with comment. Repost broadcasts as-is. Quote opens the compose sheet with the source post embedded as a card.

Once reposted, the icon goes green #00BA7C until undone. Long-press the repost button to bypass the sheet and go straight to quote.

sheet: bottom 14px reposted: #00BA7C haptic: medium on confirm
Verified Hierarchy

Blue checks, gold checks, gray checks.

Verification on X is a tiered system. Blue = X Premium subscriber. Gold = verified organization. Gray = government/multilateral. Each badge renders at the same 14pt scale next to the display name.

The badge is a checkmark inside a starburst — never just a check. The starburst makes it instantly distinguishable from a generic "verified" icon.
