iOS Surface Anatomy

Three surfaces. One unmistakable Blurple.

Discord on iOS lives in a dark canvas. The server rail is the spine. Every conversation is one tap into a server, then one tap into a channel — depth in a 390pt screen.

Servers / Channel

The 46pt server rail anchors the left edge. Active server: rounded square + 30pt white indicator. Channels tree at 96pt. Usernames render in role color: admin = red, mod = green, bot = blurple.

Direct Messages

Same server rail; the DM list takes the place of the channel tree. Status dots ride the avatar's bottom-right with a 2.5pt cutout. Unread chips in red #F23F43.

Voice Channel

2-column tile grid. Speaking tiles get a 2pt green outline. Activity strings ride bottom-left: Playing Valorant. Bottom dock = mute / deafen / video / disconnect.

Role Colors

Usernames carry the org chart.

Discord encodes hierarchy in color. The same message becomes red when sent by an admin, green by a mod, blurple by a bot, yellow by a custom role. Roles compose — the top role wins.

Role color appears on the username, the role badge pill, and (if configured) the member list border.

Status Ring

A 4pt cutout that says everything.

Status sits at 11pt on small avatars, 18pt on profile cards. The dot is solid-fill — green / yellow / red / gray — and the avatar mask carries a notch underneath so the dot reads as cut-out, not overlaid.

Custom statuses replace the dot with an emoji at the same 18×18 mask. Streaming uses the purple variant.

online: #23A559 idle: #F0B232 dnd: #F23F43 offline: #80848E
Reaction Picker

Long-press becomes a feeling.

Long-press any message and Discord lifts a Blurple-edged picker over the chat. Frequently used emoji float to the top row. Custom server emoji appear in their own tab.

Reactions render below the message as chip pills, outlined Blurple when you've reacted, gray-outlined when you haven't.
