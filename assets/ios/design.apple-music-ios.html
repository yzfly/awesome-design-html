iOS Surface Anatomy

White canvas. Pink mark. Album art does the rest.

Apple Music inherits its skeleton from iOS — large titles, grouped lists, SF Pro typography — and lets the dynamic Now Playing gradient be the only "designed" surface in the app.

Listen Now

iOS large title at 32pt/800. Horizontal Stations carousel with 78pt circular disks, then a 2×3 Recently Played grid, then a gradient hero card.

Now Playing

Modal sheet pulled up from the mini-player. Album-art-derived gradient backdrop, large square cover, white 60pt circular play, and the iconic time-synced lyrics scroll.

Library

Standard iOS section list — Playlists / Artists / Albums / Songs / Music Videos / Downloaded — with the pink chevron, then a 2-column "Recently Added" grid.

Time-synced lyrics

Karaoke that knows the beat.

Tap the lyric icon during Now Playing and the cover shrinks. Lyrics scroll past at the song's actual timestamps; the active line is 24pt/800 in full opacity, neighbors fade to 0.55, the line two-away dims to 0.35.

Tap any line to seek to its timestamp. The gradient backdrop comes from the album art's dominant hue, capped at 80% saturation.

AirPlay device picker

One sheet, every speaker in the house.

The standard iOS AirPlay sheet — translucent systemMaterialLight, 14pt corner, 50pt row height. Selected device gets the pink color treatment on its icon, name, and checkmark.

Headphones get a circle icon, HomePod gets a tonal disc, Apple TV gets the rectangular speaker glyph. Categories are uppercase 13pt at 0.6px tracking.

blur: systemMaterialLight radius: 14pt selected: #FA233B
Spatial Audio

Dolby Atmos lives at the top right.

When the playing track is mastered in Dolby Atmos, an ATMOS badge appears at the top-right of Now Playing — black gradient with a 4-bar animated equalizer that pulses with the music.

The badge becomes a button: tap it to surface the Spatial Audio settings sheet (Always On / Fixed / Off) without leaving Now Playing.
