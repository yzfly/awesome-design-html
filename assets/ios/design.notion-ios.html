iOS Surface Anatomy

Three surfaces. No color budget.

Notion's iOS app is almost monochrome on purpose. Six pastel tints exist to highlight a word or wrap a callout — never to brand a button. Hairline borders carry every structural beat.

📄

Page

Emoji icon over an H1 title, then a stream of mixed blocks: paragraphs, toggles, callouts, tables, checkboxes. Side padding stays at 18pt. Borders on blocks are 0 — only the table earns hairlines.

🗂️

Database

View tabs sit under the title like browser tabs — Board, Table, Calendar, Gallery. Kanban cards land at 4px radius, 1px border, no shadow. The active tab gets a single black underline.

🦊

Workspace Home

Sidebar drawer over a #F7F6F3 warm background. Pages disclose with a tiny arrow. Emoji-as-icon by default. Notion AI sits at the bottom in pink, the one true colored row.

Slash Commands

Type "/", get a vocabulary.

The most-used affordance in the app. Hitting "/" in any editable block lifts a floating sheet of block types — heading, toggle, callout, code, table, image — each with a 36×36 black-bordered icon tile that mirrors the desktop app pixel-for-pixel.

Arrow keys / drag select between rows on iPad with a hardware keyboard; touch select on iPhone scrolls a single column.

sheet radius: 6pt row height: 44pt icon tile: 36pt fuzzy match

Block Handle

Six dots, and the whole page rearranges.

Every block on iPhone exposes a plus + six-dot drag handle at its left edge — invisible until tap-and-hold. Long-press lifts the block with a light haptic; the rest of the page reflows around it as you drag.

Tap-only on the handle opens the block action menu: duplicate, turn into, color, move to.

handle: 14×14pt haptic: light lift: scale 1.02 shadow: y4 b12
Notion AI

One sparkle. One color the brand never uses anywhere else.

Notion AI introduces the only persistent non-neutral hue in the product — a pink sparkle. Triggered inline (space at the start of a new block) or via the sidebar entry, it presents a small bubble offering rewrite, summarize, continue, brainstorm.

Output streams character-by-character with a subtle pink underline that fades after 800ms.

Design System Reference

The least colorful system in the App Store.

Every token Notion's iOS app reaches for — the warm-black ink ladder, the six tints reserved for highlights, the radius scale that almost never exceeds 6px. Hairlines do the structural work; color does not.

Typography

Notion's UI uses a custom sans (Notion Sans) — substituted here with Inter. The page-title hierarchy goes h1 32 / h2 24 / body 16, all at 1.45–1.5 line-height. Italicized serif appears only in marketing or empty states.
