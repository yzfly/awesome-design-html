iOS Surface Anatomy

Three surfaces. One calm violet.

Every Hinge screen earns its position on an iPhone through the same rules — white canvas, slow scroll, prompt-cards stacked vertically, and a single accent purple that means "this is the one button worth pressing."

Discover

One profile at a time — full-bleed photos and prompt cards alternate down a single scrollable feed. Each card has its own #5D3F92 heart. You're not swiping; you're reading.

Likes You

2-column grid of inbound likes. Hearts tab default; Roses tab for premium. Free tier blurs every other card behind a backdrop-filter of 8px — the paywall is honest about itself.

Standouts

A daily-refresh tray of the day's best prompts, paired with a gold Rose CTA — the one currency that costs something. Plus a "We Met" follow-up loop.

Comment-on-Prompt

Reply to a specific thing, not a whole person.

Tap the floating violet heart on any prompt card and a sheet pops up with a single text input — your reply attaches to that prompt, not the profile. This is the entire conversational engine of the app.

Send button is a #5D3F92 circle; idle hearts are outlined, active hearts fill.

Rose Economy

The premium currency that actually means something.

One free Rose per week. Extra Roses cost money. Receiving one bumps you to the top of someone's Likes You queue and sends an explicit "this person stood out" signal — not a generic like.

Gold #CBA258 is the only warm color in the app — reserved entirely for the Rose system. Never used for active states, never used for CTAs.

rose: #CBA258 shadow: y6 b16 StoreKit: 1 / 3 / 12 / 50
Voice Prompts

Hear the laugh before you decide.

Profiles can host one 30-second voice answer to a Hinge prompt. The waveform renders pre-baked from AVAudioSession amplitude samples; tap to play, scrub to seek. AirPods routing is automatic.

Wave bars use the accent at opacity:.85, gaining 100% opacity as the playhead crosses each bar — visual scrubber and waveform in one element.
