iOS Surface Anatomy

Three surfaces. One unmistakable yellow.

Snapchat doesn't have brand chrome — the brand IS the chrome. Open the app and you're in the camera. Swipe right for chat. Swipe left for stories. The whole shape of the product fits inside one gesture vocabulary.

Camera

The home screen. Full-bleed black preview, a single white shutter ring, a lens carousel that lives above the button. Long-press → record. Right swipe → chat. Left swipe → stories.

Chat

Each friend gets an arrow that color-codes sent / received / opened. Streak count + flame emoji. Bitmoji is the avatar — never an initial bubble.

Stories

Friends section on top with story rings — pink/red unviewed gradient, gray when viewed. For You / Subscriptions render as 9:16 tiles with publisher tag + headline.

Shutter Button

One button, two intentions.

Tap takes a photo. Press and hold records video. The same surface — a 84pt white inner circle inside a 120pt white ring — morphs at touch-down: the ring turns red, the inner becomes a 42pt rounded square. No labels, no tooltips.

The shutter sits dead-center horizontally, 96pt above the tab bar. Haptic light on tap, haptic medium at record start.

Color-Coded Arrows

The send arrow IS the status.

Snapchat's chat row carries no text. Just an arrow and a color. Blue means text sent. Red means video. Purple means received-but-unopened. Pink means opened by you. A whole conversation state in 14 pixels.

Hollow vs solid is the second axis — sent (solid), received (solid), opened (hollow / arrow inverted).

blue: #3F6DFF red: #FF4B4B purple: #A45CFF pink: #FF77B6
Bitmoji + Friendmoji

Every avatar is already a feeling.

Snapchat's avatar isn't a photo crop — it's a Bitmoji, posed differently per context. The same friend appears reading a book in a chat, dancing in a story header, sleeping on the Snap Map. Friendmoji combines two avatars into one circle for shared moments.

Avatars always render at 50% radius. Bitmoji never gets a status dot — its pose IS the status.

radius: 50% profile: 68 × 68pt chat row: 42 × 42pt friendmoji: 96 × 68
