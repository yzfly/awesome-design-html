iOS Surface Anatomy

Three surfaces. One unmistakable green.

Every Spotify screen earns its position on an iPhone through the same rules — black canvas, soft surface ladders, pill-shaped controls, and exactly one accent that means "this is interactive."

Home

2x3 quick-access tiles above horizontal carousels. Top gradient fades from #283d2b into #121212 — a soft hand-off, no border. Chips at the top filter the feed; the active chip flips to #1ed760.

Now Playing

The album art's dominant color becomes the screen's gradient — extracted at runtime, capped at 80% saturation. White play button sits 60×60 at the optical center. Heart turns #1ed760 when saved.

Your Library

Vertical list, 52px artwork, double-line meta. Filter pills become solid white when active (inverse of Home). Mini-player floats persistent above the tab bar at #3a3a3a.

Dynamic Island

Live Activity that breathes with the track.

While Spotify plays, the island carries the album art on the left and a 7-bar waveform animation on the right — pulsing at the song's BPM. Long-press expands to scrubber + skip controls without leaving the host app.

Waveform color follows the extracted gradient. Default fallback: #1ed760.

Haptics

Every tap has a weight.

Spotify pairs visual state changes with Apple's haptic vocabulary — light for play/pause, medium for skip, success for liking a track, selection for landing a scrubber on a beat marker.

Haptics fire even on the lock screen widget. The system honors the user's iOS "Reduce Motion" preference and falls back gracefully when System Haptics are disabled.

Lock Screen · Control Center

Now Playing, anywhere iOS will host it.

The MPNowPlayingInfoCenter contract: album art, title, artist, scrubber, transport. Spotify pushes a translucent widget that adopts the system glass backdrop — no Spotify chrome at all, just metadata and white iconography.

AirPlay routing surface inherits the green accent for the selected device row only.

Typography

CircularSp family — substituted here with Inter. Bold/regular binary hierarchy. Buttons stay uppercase with 1.4–2px tracking. Numbers in scrubbers and durations use tabular figures so they don't jitter.

Radius

Pill-and-circle geometry. Buttons land at 500px / 9999px; play controls at 50%; cards stay subtle at 4–8px. The mini-player at the bottom uses only 6px — the corners want to be felt, not seen.
