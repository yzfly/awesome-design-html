If a galaxy's rotation curve is flat out to 50 kpc, what's the minimum dark-matter halo mass we should expect, and which models are still consistent at that radius?
⊛
Quick estimate: for a flat curve at ~220 km/s out to 50 kpc, enclosed mass scales as M(r) = v² r / G — giving roughly 5.6 × 10¹¹ M☉ within that radius.

Models still consistent: standard ΛCDM NFW halos with concentration c ≈ 8–12 fit cleanly. SIDM remains viable but predicts a slightly cored profile inside ~3 kpc. MOND-only solutions struggle past ~30 kpc here without an additional dark component.
