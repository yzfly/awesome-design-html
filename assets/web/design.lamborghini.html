The geometry
beneath the bull.

Lamborghini's design language is constructed from hexagons, three-armed stars, and 12° angled terminals — every surface either reflects light or absorbs it, with absolutely nothing in between. Gold (#FFC000) is rationed like fuel: only the primary action gets it.
