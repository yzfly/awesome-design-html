Core features

An extendable launcher.

Tailored to your needs. Built for community. Designed by you. Raycast is more than a launcher — it's a way to keep your hands on the keyboard.

Quicklinks

Open any webpage, query, or deep-link with a custom shortcut. Auto-fill parameters from your clipboard or selection.

⌘/
Snippets

Save text you type frequently as snippets. Trigger them with a custom keyword and watch them expand inline anywhere.

⌘⇧S
Calculator

Inline math, unit conversion, dates, currencies. Type and read the answer — no calculator app needed.

2*pi*r
Window Management

Arrange windows with keyboard shortcuts. Halves, thirds, corners, displays — all without the mouse leaving the keyboard.

⌃⌥→
AI Chat

One ⌘⏎ away from sonnet, gpt-4, and friends. Compose, summarize, translate without leaving the launcher.

⌘⏎
Open SDK

Build your own extensions with TypeScript + React. Publish to the store and share with the community in a click.
