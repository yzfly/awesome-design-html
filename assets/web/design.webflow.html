Design system reference

Tokens behind the visual canvas.

Webflow's marketing surface is a near-black-on-white system punctuated by a five-stop chromatic palette. Cards take a tight 8 px radius; buttons take 4 px. The brand never goes pill, and never goes above weight 600.
