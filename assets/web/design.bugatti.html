The Bugatti language.

Three custom typefaces in rigid trinity: Display (uppercase wide-tracked, here substituted with Saira Condensed), Text Regular (serif body, here EB Garamond), Monospace (buttons, captions, nav, here JetBrains Mono). Typography · The trinity

A rigid, three-family system. Bugatti Display (substituted with Saira Condensed) for all uppercase headlines and the wordmark. Bugatti Text Regular (substituted with EB Garamond) for body in serif italic — the literary voice. Bugatti Monospace (substituted with JetBrains Mono) for buttons, captions, navigation. Never bold — emphasis comes from size, tracking, case and family — never weight.
