Typography

Plain Black is Clay's licensed display face — Fraunces (variable, SOFT axis at 100) is the open-source substitute that carries the rounded character. Display weight stays at 500 — going to 700 reads as bombastic. Inter handles body, UI, and buttons. Mixing the two between roles is a system violation.
