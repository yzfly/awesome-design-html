— Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need to know code?

No. The canvas is the artifact — there's nothing to export.

Can I use my own domain?

Pro and above include custom domain mapping with auto-HTTPS.

How do collaborators work?

Business adds 5 editor seats with roles and permissions; Enterprise scales to unlimited with SSO.

What happens to my site if I cancel?

It moves to a Frameworks subdomain. You keep editing on Basic, or export the published HTML at any time.

Typography

GT Walsheim Medium is Frameworks's proprietary display face — Manrope at weight 500 is the closest open-source substitute, with negative letter-spacing applied as a percentage of size (~5% at display, ~1% at body). Inter Variable handles the body, with character variants cv05, cv09, cv11 and stylistic sets ss03, ss07 enabled.
