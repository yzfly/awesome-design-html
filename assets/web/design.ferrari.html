Typography

FerrariSans (Inter substitute) at modest weights — display 500, body 400. Display sizes carry negative letter-spacing (-0.36 to -1.6px). CTA labels are uppercase with 1.4px tracking. Display weight never goes bold — the cinematic photography does the heavy lifting.
