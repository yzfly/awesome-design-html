Typography

figmaSans is exercised at unusually fine variable weight increments — Inter is the open-source substitute, with line-heights nudged ~0.02 down to compensate for taller x-height. figmaMono (JetBrains Mono sub) is reserved for eyebrows and captions, always uppercase, positive tracking.
